Rules

The object of the game is to score as many points as possible. You score by rolling certain combinations of dice.

Begin by rolling the dice and then selecting at least one die to keep. The dice that are not kept can be rolled again. The dice that are selected to be kept must individually contribute to the score or form a scoring combination with other kept dice. If no scoring dice are rolled, zero points are awarded and a new attempt must begin i.e. all six dice must be rolled

Some dice score, and can be kept, individually
V’s 5 points per die
X’s 10 points per die

Rolling four or more V’s results in the player’s entire score being reset to zero.

Some dice only score, and can only be kept, in groups of three
3 L’s 50 points per group
3 C’s 100 points per group
3 D’s 500 points per group
3 M’s 1000 points per group
Some scoring combinations require all six dice
Six of a kind 5000 points
One of each 2000 points
Three pairs 1000 points
